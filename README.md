# Navigators

Live: https://laurencefwhite.github.io/navigators/

This repository holds the published site only.

## Credits

Every journey's card lists its sources, with links. Quotations are from public-domain texts.

- Coastlines, ice shelves, lakes, rivers and shaded relief: Natural Earth (public domain).
- d3-geo, d3-array, d3-color (ISC) and topojson-client (BSD).
- Period maps: public-domain scans from Wikimedia Commons, the Library of Congress, the National Library of
  Australia and others; each map's card links its source.
- The music and sounds are synthesised in the browser; no recordings are used.
